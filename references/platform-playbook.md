# Platform playbook

Per-platform formats, limits and habits. Treat these as strong defaults, not
laws; check the platform if something looks out of date.

## LinkedIn

- Best for: professional insight, story, opinion, lessons learned, hiring.
- Sweet spot: 900-1,300 characters; the first 2 lines are the whole hook.
- Format: short paragraphs, one idea each, white space between them. No external
  link in the body - put it in the first comment.
- Hashtags: 0-3, at the end.
- Best posting: weekday mornings, local time.
- Avoid: engagement-bait, links in the post body, walls of text.

## X (Twitter)

- Best for: sharp takes, threads, news reactions, quick value.
- A single post is ~280 characters; a thread is 5-10 posts, each standing alone.
- Hook in post 1; end with a clear CTA or a reason to follow.
- Hashtags: 0-1. Links: expect reduced reach.

## Instagram

- Best for: visual brand, behind-the-scenes, Reels, carousels, stories.
- Caption: 125 characters visible before "more"; lead with the hook.
- Carousel: 5-10 slides, one point each. Reels: hook in the first 1-2 seconds.
- Hashtags: 3-10 relevant ones, or none if the account is niche.
- Media is required for publishing via most connectors - create the post as a
  draft, attach media, then schedule.

## Facebook

- Best for: community, longer story, local audience, events.
- Posts: 1-2 short paragraphs. Native video and images beat links.
- Groups often outperform the page for reach.

## YouTube

- Best for: search-driven how-to, reviews, long-form trust.
- Title under ~60 characters; description's first 2 lines matter; thumbnail is
  half the click.
- Shorts for reach, long-form for depth.

## TikTok

- Best for: reach, trends, entertainment, fast how-to.
- Hook in the first second; vertical video; on-screen text; trending sound.
- Caption short, 3-5 hashtags.

## Pinterest

- Best for: evergreen discovery - recipes, design, how-to, products.
- Pins are images; the description carries keywords.
- Note: some connectors list Pinterest as connect-only or read-only - check
  before promising a publish.

## Threads

- Best for: casual, conversational, text-first.
- Short, opinionated posts; reply to others a lot.

## Bluesky

- Best for: text-first, tech and news communities.
- Similar to X but lighter; short posts, custom feeds matter.

## Mastodon

- Best for: text-first, community-run, tech and open-source.
- Use content warnings and hashtags (they are the discovery mechanism).

## Reddit

- Best for: genuine participation in a niche community.
- Rules matter more than reach: read the subreddit rules first. No hard selling.
- Post as a person, not a brand, unless the community allows otherwise.

## Discord

- Best for: community management, announcements, support.
- Use channels and threads well; keep announcements short and useful.

## Telegram

- Best for: broadcast to an owned audience, communities.
- Short, frequent, clear.

## Google Business Profile

- Best for: local discovery - updates, offers, hours, reviews.
- Keep posts short and local; reviews matter most.

## Twitch

- Best for: live community; titles and categories drive discovery.

## Cross-platform rules

- Do not post identical text everywhere; adapt the format to each platform.
- Keep the user's voice consistent across all of them.
- One clear CTA per post.
- Reuse ideas, not copy: one idea can become a thread, a carousel, a short and a
  newsletter section, each rewritten for its home.
